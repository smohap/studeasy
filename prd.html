01

Overview & Problem Statement

Most tutoring businesses run on a website that only markets lessons — bookings happen by phone or form, homework is emailed, progress is reported informally, and every worksheet is written by hand. This limits how many students a tutor can serve well, and gives parents little visibility into whether their investment is working.

TutorWise turns a single-location tutoring business into a platform: a marketing site that converts visitors into trial bookings, plus a Student, Parent, Tutor and Admin portal built around one core idea — an AI Learning Twin that knows each student's curriculum, mistakes and pace well enough to personalise their path to Excellence.

the platform IS the differentiator — not the marketing site around it.

Users & Personas

Student — "Aroha, Year 11"

Studying towards NCEA Level 1 Mathematics. Wants quick, judgement-free help at 9pm on a homework question, and to know exactly what's costing her marks.

Parent — "David"

Pays for two children's lessons. Wants a plain-English monthly update, not a spreadsheet of scores, and an easy way to pay and reschedule.

Tutor — "Ms. Patel"

Teaches 25 students across Maths and Physics. Spends evenings writing worksheets and progress notes by hand. Wants that time back without losing her teaching style.

Admin — "the Founder"

Runs the business. Needs one place to see revenue, tutor utilisation, churn risk and marketing performance.

Public Site Structure

Home · About · Courses · Subjects · Tutors · Pricing · Book Free Assessment · Success Stories · Resources · Blog · FAQ · Contact · Login

Functional requirements

  • Home page must include a free diagnostic/assessment booking CTA above the fold.
  • Subject pages are dynamic — driven by curriculum data (NCEA L1–L3, Cambridge, IB, IGCSE, CBSE) so new subjects/levels don't require a code change.
  • Success Stories page pulls anonymised before/after grade data from the platform (with parent consent) rather than being manually written.
  • SEO-optimised static rendering for Home, Subjects, Blog, and Tutor bio pages (Next.js SSR/ISR).
05

Student Portal

Dashboard

Single view of today's lesson, homework due, upcoming tests, attendance, study streak, and AI-recommended practice, ordered by urgency.

AI Study Coach Killer feature

Conversational Q&A grounded in the student's curriculum, prior mistakes and weak topics, and — critically — trained on the tutor's own worksheets and explanations, not a generic model answer.

Personal Learning Path

AI-generated pipeline: Current Level → Weak Areas → Weekly Plan → Lessons → Practice → Tests → Improvement, recalculated after every graded activity.

Homework, Practice Generator & AI Doubt Solver

  • Upload homework as photo, PDF, Word or PowerPoint; system flags missing/incomplete work and suggests similar practice questions.
  • Practice generator: student selects topic, difficulty, question count, curriculum and time budget; AI returns MCQs, long-answer, word problems, graphs and geometry questions.
  • Doubt solver: student uploads a screenshot or photo of a problem and receives a step-by-step explanation plus similar worked examples — available 24/7.

Interactive Whiteboard & Lesson Recording

Live-class whiteboard (draw, shapes, equations, graphs, saved notes). Every lesson is recorded and indexed so a student can search a term (e.g. "Quadratic Formula") and jump straight to that timestamp.

06

Parent Portal

Dashboard: attendance, homework, test scores, tutor comments, invoices, progress, study hours, upcoming classes.

AI Parent Report Killer feature

Replaces generic percentage reports with a short, plain-English narrative generated after each lesson/term — e.g. what improved, what's still shaky, and what to expect next — plus monthly charts of accuracy, homework completion, attendance, confidence and time spent.

07

Tutor Portal

Dashboard: today's schedule, assignments, students, messages, reports, one-click attendance (Present / Late / Absent).

AI Lesson Planner & Worksheet Generator Killer feature

Tutor types a topic (e.g. "Year 10 Algebra") and receives objectives, activities, worked examples, a quiz and homework in seconds. Unlimited worksheet, quiz, exam and revision-sheet generation, all in the tutor's own style once trained on their materials.

Student Notes & In-lesson AI Assistant

Auto-generated lesson summaries (strengths, weaknesses, behaviour, homework) and quiet in-lesson suggestions to the tutor — better explanations, examples, diagrams, extra questions — without interrupting the class.

08

Admin Portal

Manage students, tutors, courses, payments, reports, emails, announcements, coupons and certificates from one console, with Power BI-backed analytics on utilisation, revenue and churn risk.

09

Booking & Payments

  • Parents choose subject, tutor, location (online/face-to-face) and available time slot, pay online, and receive instant confirmation.
  • Payment methods: Stripe, POLi, credit card, bank transfer. Invoices generated automatically per lesson or subscription cycle.

11

Gamification

XP, coins, badges, ranks and certificates layered over real academic milestones: daily streak, weekly challenge, monthly champion, subject mastery, quiz battles, house points, avatar upgrades and a reward shop. Gamification should reward effort and consistency (streaks, completion) at least as much as raw accuracy, to avoid discouraging struggling students.

Risks & Open Questions

AI gives an answer that contradicts a tutor's methodGround all AI responses in the academy's own worksheets/notes; tutor can flag & correct answers, feeding back into the model.
Parents distrust AI-written progress reportsEvery AI report is reviewable and editable by the tutor before it's sent; label as "AI-drafted, tutor-reviewed."
