15

Non-Functional Requirements

CategoryRequirement
Multi-org scaleFeature flags and branding must be resolvable per-request with no measurable latency penalty vs. MVP single-tenant.
AI trustAll AI-generated marking/reports carry an "AI-drafted, teacher-reviewed" label until explicitly approved.
Data residencyOrg-level configuration for data region, needed before onboarding non-NZ organizations.
Integration securityOAuth-scoped, revocable per-integration tokens (Drive, Zoom, Xero, etc.), audited in the existing audit log.
AccessibilityWCAG 2.1 AA across all new Phase 1 surfaces, not just MVP ones.
16

Sequencing Within Phase 1

1 — AI content & marking

AI homework marking, lesson planner, worksheet generator, PDF→content pipeline. Delivers the biggest teacher time-savings first.

2 — Whiteboard & live teaching tools

Interactive whiteboard, recording metadata, calendar sync.

3 — Organization & integrations

Branding, custom domains, billing, Drive/Zoom/Xero — unlocks selling StudEasy to a second organization.

4 — Predictive analytics

At-risk detection, engagement heatmaps, executive dashboards — needs a full term of Phase 1 data to be meaningful.
